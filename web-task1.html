Healthy Recipes

Vegetable Salad

Ingredients: Cucumber, tomato, carrot and lettuce.

Preparation: Wash and chop vegetables. Mix together.

Approx. 180 kcal per serving

Healthy Breakfast Bowl

Ingredients: Oats, milk, banana and nuts.

Preparation: Cook oats with milk and add fruits.

Approx. 300 kcal per serving

Vegetable Soup

Ingredients: Carrot, beans, tomato and spinach.

Preparation: Cook vegetables with water and spices.

Approx. 150 kcal per serving

Fresh Fruit Salad

Ingredients: Apple, banana, papaya and grapes.

Preparation: Cut fruits and mix gently.

Approx. 160 kcal per serving

Daily Healthy Meal Plan

Breakfast: Idli, sambar and fruit.

Morning Snack: Seasonal fruit and nuts.

Lunch: Rice, dal, vegetables and curd.

Evening Snack: Milk or sprouts.

Dinner: Chapati, vegetable curry and dal.

Important Nutrients

Protein

Helps build and repair body tissues.

Sources: Eggs, pulses and milk.

Carbohydrates

Provide energy for daily activities.

Sources: Rice, wheat and oats.

Healthy Fats

Support normal body functions.

Sources: Nuts, seeds and oils.

Vitamins

Support normal growth and health.

Sources: Fruits and vegetables.

Healthy Eating Tips

1. Eat a variety of fruits and vegetables.

2. Drink enough water throughout the day.

3. Include different food groups in meals.

4. Choose whole grains regularly.

5. Maintain regular meal timings.

6. Limit excess salt and sugary drinks.
